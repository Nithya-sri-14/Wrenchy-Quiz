About Wrenchy Quiz

An AI-powered interactive quiz platform by Wrenchy Quiz. Built with Node.js, Express, and Gemini AI. Version 2.0.

Rules & Guidelines

  • Quick Slices: Each quiz session contains exactly 5 questions filtered by your selected category and difficulty level.
  • Active Clock: You have a maximum of 20 seconds to solve each question.
  • Sound warning: The timer will trigger rapid warnings and alert ticks during the final 5 seconds.
  • Points Structure:
    • Correct Answer: +20 Marks
    • Incorrect / Skipped / Timeout: 0 Marks
  • Retro Synthesizer: Web Audio synthesizers supply live quiz sound effects. Toggle audio using the speaker icon.
  • Leaderboard Ranking: Top players ranked by final score. Faster times break ties!
